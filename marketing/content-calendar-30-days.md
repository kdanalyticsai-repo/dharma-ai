# DharmaAI — 30-Day Content Calendar 🪔

A ready-to-run plan to grow DharmaAI organically. Built for **Instagram + Facebook Reels** (your #1 channel) and **YouTube Shorts** (re-upload the same vertical videos), with carousels and a few LinkedIn/X posts mixed in.

**Goal of the first 30 days:** consistency, find your 2–3 breakout formats, and seed a following. Most "overnight" spiritual accounts grinded daily for ~90 days first — treat this month as building the engine.

> **"Ask the Bhagavad Gita anything."** — your AI spiritual guide. Gita, Vedas & Upanishads, in your language.

- **Bio link:** `dharma.kdaanalytics.com/welcome`

## Cadence & posting times (IST)
- **1 Reel/day** (the minimum that works). Cross-post to FB Reels + YouTube Shorts same day.
- **Best times:** 7–9 AM (morning wisdom) and 8–10 PM (evening reflection).
- **2 carousels/week**, **2–3 Stories/day** (polls, "ask the Gita", behind-the-scenes).
- **LinkedIn/X:** 2–3×/week (founder + build-in-public).

## The 5 content pillars
1. **Daily Wisdom** — one verse + a modern, relatable take.
2. **Ask AI Guru** — screen-record real life questions → the app's answer. *(Your signature, most shareable format.)*
3. **Problem → Dharma** — start with a relatable pain, end with the answer + app.
4. **Build / Founder** — the story of an AI that answers life's questions via the Gita.
5. **Festivals & Moments** — Ekadashi, Janmashtami, Gita Jayanti, etc. (huge organic spikes).

## Production tips (keep it sustainable)
- **Batch:** film/record 7 Reels in one sitting; schedule via Meta Business Suite.
- **First 1.5 seconds decide everything** — open with the hook text on screen + a pattern interrupt.
- **Subtitles always** (most watch muted). Use trending, calm audio where it fits.
- **Reuse:** one verse → a Reel + a carousel + a Story + a tweet. 1 idea = 4 posts.
- **AI Guru demos are your edge** — they show the product *and* deliver value. Make 2–3/week.
- **Reply to every comment** in the first hour (boosts reach massively).
- **Watch the data after ~2 weeks:** double down on whatever 2 formats over-perform; drop the rest.

## What to track
- Followers, reach, **saves & shares** (saves/shares > likes for this niche), profile-link taps, and DharmaAI signups (Firebase Analytics).
