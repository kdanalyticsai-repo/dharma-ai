Refund Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

1. Subscriptions do not auto-renew

DharmaAI paid plans (Monthly, Quarterly, Annual) are one-time purchases for the stated period. They do not automatically renew, so there is no recurring charge to cancel. Your premium access simply ends at the end of the period unless you choose to purchase again.

2. Cancellation

Because there is no auto-renewal, no cancellation action is required to avoid future charges. You may stop using premium features at any time; access remains available until the end of your paid period.

3. Refunds

DharmaAI delivers digital content and AI services that are accessible immediately after purchase. As such:

  • Payments are generally non-refundable once premium access has been used.
  • If you were charged in error (e.g., a duplicate payment or a failed transaction that was still debited), please contact us within 7 days and we will investigate and refund verified erroneous charges to the original payment method.
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days via Razorpay to the original payment method.

4. How to request

Email info@kdaanalytics.com with your registered email address and the payment reference (Razorpay payment ID). We aim to respond within 2 business days.

This policy is provided in good faith and in line with our payment provider's requirements. Nothing here limits any non-waivable rights you may have under applicable consumer-protection law.
