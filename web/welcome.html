Frequently asked questions

 

What is DharmaAI?

DharmaAI is a free AI spiritual companion. Ask an AI Guru for guidance grounded in the Bhagavad Gita, Vedas and Upanishads, read scripture with translations and commentary, receive a daily verse and audio reading, and track your daily sadhana.

Is DharmaAI free?

Yes — DharmaAI is free to use, with an optional premium membership for unlimited AI guidance and the full audio library.

Which scriptures does it cover?

The Bhagavad Gita, the Vedas and the Upanishads — with Sanskrit, transliteration, translations and commentary.

What languages are supported?

English, Hindi, Tamil and Bengali — across the interface, translations, AI guidance and emails.

Can I ask the AI about my own life?

Yes. The AI Guru offers compassionate, personalised guidance for everyday concerns — work, anxiety, purpose, relationships — drawing on the wisdom of the scriptures.
