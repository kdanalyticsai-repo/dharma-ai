Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

This policy is operated by KDAA Analytics Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "DharmaAI"), located in the Delhi NCR Region, India, for the DharmaAI application and website at dharma.kdaanalytics.com.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register, and your profile photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Spiritual activity: your bookmarks, sadhana (practice) progress, journal notes, and community (Sangha) posts that you create.
  • AI interactions: the questions you ask the AI Scripture Scholar and AI Guru. Answers to common scripture questions may be cached in anonymized, hashed form (the raw question text is not stored).
  • Subscription & payment: your plan and payment status. Card/UPI details are processed directly by our payment provider (Razorpay) and are never stored on our servers.
  • Technical data: basic device/browser information and locally-stored preferences (via your browser's local storage).

2. How we use your information

  • To provide and personalize the app (greeting, language, saved content, progress).
  • To generate AI-based spiritual guidance you request.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock premium features.
  • To maintain security and improve the service.

3. Service providers we share data with

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting.
  • Cloudflare — application hosting and a secure proxy for AI requests.
  • OpenAI — generating AI responses (your prompt is sent to produce a reply).
  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • Google — optional Google Sign-In.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data retention

We retain your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact).

5. Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data, and withdraw consent, by contacting us. We will respond within a reasonable period as required by applicable law.

6. Children

DharmaAI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will reflect changes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests: info@kdaanalytics.com.
